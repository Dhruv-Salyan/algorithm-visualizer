02 · WHY IT HELPS

Why visualize instead of just reading code

Pseudocode tells you what happens. A visualization shows you why it works.

Intuition

Builds real intuition

Watching pointers converge or a search space shrink makes the invariant behind the algorithm obvious, not just memorized.

Debugging

Exposes edge cases

Off-by-one errors and boundary conditions are far easier to spot when you can see the exact index a pointer sits on.

Retention

Sticks in memory longer

A pattern you've watched play out visually is easier to recall in an interview than one you only read once in a textbook.
